PROFILE

Turning the uncertain into the verifiable.

My path crosses three worlds: game design and production in a game team, app product management in the internet industry, then three years running a full supply chain in the physical-goods industry - category planning, factory sourcing and sampling, process sign-off, mass-production delivery and channel materials. Those years taught me one thing: no matter how good the idea, it only counts once it has been through real-world execution.

Today I am the sole founder and developer of RanxiHub.AI. I do not treat AI as a stand-in for an outsourced writer, but as an extension of product judgement: needs are broken into flows, flows are built into systems, systems are shipped and validated, and feedback feeds the next round. Product definition, architecture, model engineering and delivery are all handled by the same person, end to end.

CONVICTIONS

Three independent judgements

These judgements underpin every RanxiHub product decision, and also determine what I choose not to build.

01

Memory is the operating-system layer of an Agent

People choose an Agent not for its parameter count, but for "does it remember me". Memory sets the usable boundary of an Agent.

02

Selling tokens is not a sustainable model

Deep service and real-world deployment are the core problem; tools have to survive the execution floor, not stop at a demo.

03

The next frontier for AI is the physical world

On-device models, cross-device memory, general IoT models and cross-device scheduling are the natural extension of memory infrastructure.
