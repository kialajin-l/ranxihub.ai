DIRECTION

From one-off generation to long-form creation.

When a story grows into long-form or multi-episode creation, characters drift, settings conflict, and approved visual assets are hard to reuse later. FrameTale's direction is to turn these continuity requirements into structured state within the workflow, rather than relying on creators to paste ever-longer prompts.

The product will first validate whether the tool itself works, and only later consider longer-term asset licensing and a creator ecosystem. It makes no early commitments to marketplace, transaction, or community features.

01

Narrative continuity

Maintains character, worldbuilding, and event state so later chapters and shots inherit story facts already established.

02

Creative asset memory

Builds reusable baselines for characters, style, and storyboards, reducing repeated work across projects and long production cycles.

03

Multi-stage workflow

Connects the idea, text, storyboard, and video stages so each step's output becomes a reliable input to the next.

CURRENT STATUS / BUILDING

FrameTale's storyboard pipeline is running end-to-end and is in closed beta: Shot Contract, Asset Memory, Consistency Gate, and the repair / replay / audit chain are in place. The current build is for closed-beta testers only; the service is not yet generally available. This page describes the product direction and the capabilities validated so far.
